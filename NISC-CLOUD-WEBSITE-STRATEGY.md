## Purpose and working agreement

Build an established, distinctive end-to-end cloud, development and marketing company website. Improve usability, accessibility, performance, service content, organic discovery, AI-answer visibility and conversion through checkout. Preserve recoverable search equity from the previous website.

## UX, design and conversion direction

Use an intentional grid, a consistent type hierarchy, Nisc Cloud brand colours, original diagrams of actual workflows, real product screenshots and authorised project imagery. Vary page composition according to purpose rather than repeating identical icon-card sections. Avoid decorative complexity that obscures the service, price or next action.

Two primary customer journeys: self-service infrastructure purchase and consultation-led development/marketing engagement. Keep both easy to find. Do not force a hosting buyer into a sales call or imply custom development is an instant commodity purchase.

Selling-hook hypotheses to validate with real offers:
- One accountable team across build, hosting and growth.
- Migration with a documented plan and clear ownership.
- Digital systems tied to operational outcomes.
- Marketing evaluated against qualified enquiries and sales.
- Transparent recurring charges, renewal terms and support scope.

Each service page should cover audience, problem, deliverables, exclusions, process, pricing basis, timeline, supporting work, genuinely useful questions and the correct next step. Answer concrete questions in plain language. Build original comparisons and guides from real expertise; avoid thin location pages, mass-produced near-duplicates or unsupported superlatives.

Checkout acceptance checklist: clear registration versus renewal versus transfer pricing; billing term and tax/currency visibility before payment; useful search/no-results/loading/error states; domain/hosting configuration preserved through login and back navigation; no preselected unwanted extras; accessible labels and validation; recoverable payment cancellation/timeouts; duplicate-payment prevention; accurate confirmation and next steps. Test with authorised sandbox payment flows, not live charges.

Performance targets to validate: mobile field p75 LCP <=2.5s, INP <=200ms and CLS <=0.1. Run representative lab tests before field data is available and label the distinction. Test keyboard use, focus, contrast, zoom, touch layouts and reduced motion. WCAG 2.2 AA is a design/testing target, not a claimed certification.
