## Smarter media spend for better results

**Nisc Cloud Media Buying** helps you get more value from your advertising budget. We analyse your audience, recommend the right mix of channels, and manage the booking and optimisation of your media placements.

![Service images](/images/ns-img-397.png)

## What’s included

Support from planning to reporting, across online and offline channels.

- **Audience and channel planning** – Decide where your ads should appear.
- **Budget planning** – Allocate spend based on goals and timelines.
- **Negotiation and booking** – Work with publishers and platforms on your behalf.
- **Campaign setup** – Ensure creatives, targeting, and tracking are in place.
- **Ongoing optimisation** – Shift budgets to the best-performing placements.
- **End‑of‑campaign reporting** – Clear summaries of reach, impact, and learnings.

![Service images](/images/ns-img-398.png)

## Who it’s for

Best suited for organisations that invest regularly in advertising.

- **Consumer brands** – Run campaigns across radio, digital, outdoor, and more.
- **Service providers** – Reach new audiences with targeted campaigns.
- **Events and launches** – Promote key dates with strong reach.
- **Institutions and NGOs** – Spread awareness of programmes and initiatives.
- **Growing businesses** – Move from small, random ads to planned media buying.
