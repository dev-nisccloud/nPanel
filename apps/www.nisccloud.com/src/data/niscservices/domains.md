## Simple domain management for your business

Your domain name is the front door to your brand online. With **Nisc Cloud Domains**, you can search, register, and transfer domains in a few easy steps. We support both global extensions like .com and local Kenyan domains such as .ke, .co.ke, and more, so your business feels local and trusted.

![Service images](/images/ns-img-397.png)

## What’s included

Everything you need to keep your domains secure, organised, and always online.

- **Fast domain search & registration** – Find a name that fits your brand in seconds.
- **Easy domain transfers** – Move your existing domains to Nisc Cloud with guided steps.
- **Local .ke domain support** – Register and manage .ke, .co.ke, and other Kenyan extensions.
- **Automatic renewals** – Avoid downtime with renewals that run on their own.
- **Domain privacy options** – Hide your personal contact details from public records where supported.
- **Simple DNS management** – Point your domain to websites, email, and other services with clear tools.

## Who it’s for

This service is built for anyone who wants a professional online identity without technical headaches.

- **Small businesses** – Secure a trusted name for your shop, office, or practice.

- **Growing brands** – Protect your brand name across different extensions.
- **Startups** – Launch quickly with a domain that matches your idea.
