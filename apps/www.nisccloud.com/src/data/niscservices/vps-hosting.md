## More power and control for serious workloads

When your projects outgrow basic hosting, **Nisc Cloud VPS Hosting** gives you isolated resources and deeper control without the cost of a full dedicated server. It’s ideal for custom applications, busy websites, and teams that need room to grow.

![Service images](/images/ns-img-397.png)

## What’s included

Built to handle demanding workloads with stability and flexibility.

- **Guaranteed resources** – Dedicated CPU, RAM, and storage for your VPS.
- **Full root or admin access** – Install and configure the software you need.
- **Scalable plans** – Upgrade resources as your traffic or data grows.
- **Secure networking** – Firewalls and access controls to protect your servers.
- **Snapshot and backup options** – Recover quickly from mistakes or failures.
- **Choice of operating systems** – Select the environment that suits your stack.

## Who it’s for

VPS Hosting is ideal for teams that have outgrown shared hosting.

- **Growing online platforms** – Handle more users and complex features.
- **Agencies & developers** – Run multiple client projects on separate servers.
- **SaaS and internal apps** – Host custom tools built for your business.
- **Large WordPress or eCommerce sites** – Improve performance and reliability.
- **IT teams** – Test, stage, and run production workloads in the cloud.
