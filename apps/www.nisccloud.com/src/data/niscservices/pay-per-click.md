## Targeted ads that you can measure

With **Nisc Cloud Pay Per Click management**, we set up and manage ads that appear when people search for your services or browse related content. You pay only when someone clicks, and we work to make every click more valuable.

![Service images](/images/ns-img-397.png)

## What’s included

We manage PPC from strategy to daily optimisation.

- **Campaign and keyword strategy** – Choose where and when your ads appear.
- **Account and campaign setup** – Configure campaigns on Google and other platforms.
- **Ad copy and creatives** – Write clear, compelling messages that match your offer.
- **Conversion tracking** – Measure leads, sales, or key actions, not just clicks.
- **Bid and budget optimisation** – Adjust bids to get better results from your spend.
- **Regular reports** – Simple updates that show what is working and what we are improving.

![Service images](/images/ns-img-398.png)

## Who it’s for

PPC is ideal for businesses that want fast, measurable results.

- **B2B and B2C companies** – Drive leads to sales teams or online checkouts.
- **Service providers** – Capture high‑intent searches in your niche.
- **E‑commerce stores** – Promote key products and offers.
- **Event organisers** – Boost registrations within a set time window.
- **Startups** – Test messages and offers while building long‑term channels like SEO.
