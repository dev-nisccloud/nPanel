## Professional email that matches your brand

With **Nisc Cloud Business Email**, your team can use addresses like info@yourbusiness.co.ke instead of free, generic accounts. This builds trust with customers and keeps your communication organised and secure.

![Service images](/images/ns-img-397.png)

## What’s included

Everything you need to run business email without stress.

- **Branded email addresses** – Use your own domain for every mailbox.
- **Web and mobile access** – Check email from any device, anywhere.
- **Secure delivery** – Built-in spam filters and protection tools.
- **Shared calendars and contacts** – Keep your team in sync.
- **Simple admin panel** – Add, remove, or reset accounts easily.
- **Reliable storage** – Generous mail storage with backup in the cloud.

## Who it’s for

Business Email is great for any organisation that wants to look professional and stay organised.

- **Growing businesses** – Give each department or staff member a proper email address.
- **Startups** – Build trust with investors and customers from day one.
- **Agencies & consultants** – Use branded email for client communication.
- **Schools & NGOs** – Communicate clearly with students, staff, and partners.
- **Online shops** – Separate support, orders, and marketing inboxes.
