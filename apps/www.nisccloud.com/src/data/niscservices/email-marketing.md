## Email campaigns made simple

With **Nisc Cloud Email Marketing**, you can create and send email campaigns without needing a designer or developer. Our drag-and-drop email builder lets you put together clean, modern emails in minutes, so you can focus on your message instead of the tech.

![Service images](/images/ns-img-397.png)

## What’s included

Everything you need to plan, design, send, and measure email campaigns.

- **Drag-and-drop email builder** – Build emails by moving blocks, not writing code.
- **Ready-made templates** – Start quickly with layouts for newsletters, offers, and updates.
- **Audience lists and segments** – Group subscribers by interest, activity, or customer type.
- **Automated campaigns** – Welcome sequences, follow-ups, and reminders that run on their own.
- **Detailed reporting** – See opens, clicks, and key actions in simple dashboards.
- **Compliance tools** – Easy unsubscribe links and list management to keep you compliant.

![Service images](/images/ns-img-398.png)

## Who it’s for

Email Marketing works for any organisation that wants to stay in touch with its audience.

- **Online shops** – Share new products, promotions, and abandoned cart reminders.
- **Service businesses** – Send updates, tips, and offers to past and current clients.
- **Schools and NGOs** – Share news, events, and impact stories with supporters.
- **B2B companies** – Nurture leads with regular, helpful content.
- **Creators and coaches** – Build communities and stay top of mind with your audience.
