## Dedicated resources for mission-critical systems

With **Nisc Cloud Dedicated Hosting**, you get an entire physical server reserved for your business. This gives you the highest level of performance, security, and customisation — ideal for applications where uptime and control are non‑negotiable.

![Service images](/images/ns-img-397.png)

## What’s included

Everything you need to host large, sensitive, or high-traffic workloads.

- **Dedicated hardware** – No sharing of CPU, RAM, or storage with other customers.
- **Custom configurations** – Tailor the server to your performance and security needs.
- **Advanced security options** – Firewalls, access controls, and monitoring.
- **High availability options** – Redundancy and failover where required.
- **Managed support options** – Get help with setup, updates, and monitoring.
- **Scalable connectivity** – Bandwidth suited for heavy traffic and data transfer.

![Service images](/images/ns-img-398.png)

## Who it’s for

Dedicated Hosting is designed for organisations with serious performance or compliance needs.

- **Financial institutions** – Host core systems and secure applications.
- **Large eCommerce platforms** – Handle heavy traffic and transactions smoothly.
- **Enterprises** – Run ERP, CRM, and other line-of-business systems.
- **Government and public sector** – Meet strict data and security requirements.
- **SaaS providers** – Offer stable performance to large customer bases.
