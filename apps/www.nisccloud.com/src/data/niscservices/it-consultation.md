## Practical IT guidance for real businesses

With **Nisc Cloud IT Consultation**, you get a partner who can translate technical options into simple choices. We review your current systems, listen to your goals, and recommend a roadmap that fits your budget and team capacity.

![Service images](/images/ns-img-397.png)

## What’s included

We focus on clarity, security, and long‑term value.

- **IT and infrastructure audit** – Understand what you have today and where the risks are.
- **Cloud and hosting recommendations** – Decide what should move to the cloud and how.
- **Security and backup planning** – Improve protection, access control, and recovery plans.
- **Network and access review** – Check how your staff, branches, or partners connect.
- **Tool and software review** – Identify overlaps, gaps, and better alternatives.
- **Roadmap and action plan** – Get a clear, prioritised list of next steps.

## Who it’s for

IT Consultation is ideal for organisations that want to modernise without wasting budget.

- **Growing SMEs** – Move from ad‑hoc tools to a clearer IT structure.
- **Enterprises** – Align IT projects with business strategy and compliance.
- **Healthcare, education, and public sector** – Improve security and continuity.
- **Multi‑branch businesses** – Standardise systems across locations.
- **Startups** – Set up a strong, flexible foundation from the beginning.
