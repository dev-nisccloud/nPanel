**Webmail** lets you use email without installing an app  open a URL in Chrome, Safari, or Firefox, sign in with your mailbox password, and read mail anywhere.

---

## Find your webmail link

1. Sign in at [npanel.nisccloud.com](https://npanel.nisccloud.com).
2. Click **Emails** → your **email plan**.
3. Look for **Webmail**, **Open webmail**, or a URL such as `https://webmail.yourdomain.com` or a Nisc Cloud webmail hostname.
4. Click the link  webmail opens in a new tab.

The exact URL is shown on your plan page or in your welcome email.

---

## Log in to webmail

1. Open the webmail URL.
2. Enter your full email address (`you@yourdomain.com`).
3. Enter your **mailbox password** (set when you [created the mailbox](/docs/email/setup)).
4. Click **Login** or **Sign in**.

You see inbox, sent mail, drafts, and compose  similar to GmailNisc Cloud's layout.

---

## Send an email

1. Click **Compose** or **New message**.
2. Enter **To**, **Subject**, and body text.
3. Attach files if needed (size limits apply).
4. Click **Send**.

---

## Common webmail tasks

| Task | Steps |
|------|--------|
| Change password | Often under **Settings → Password** in webmail, or reset in dashboard **Emails** |
| Set signature | **Settings → Identities** or **Signature** |
| Create folder | Right sidebar → **Folders → Create** |
| Search mail | Use search bar at top |

---

## When webmail does not load

1. Confirm mailbox exists under **Emails** in dashboard.
2. Check [DNS MX records](/docs/email/dns-records) are correct.
3. Try a private/incognito browser window.
4. Verify SSL certificate on webmail hostname  see [SSL](/docs/ssl).

For daily use on phone or desktop, set up [Email clients](/docs/email/clients) instead.
