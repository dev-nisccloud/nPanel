title: Set up mailboxes

---

This guide shows how to **create mailboxes** (email accounts) on your domain after you have ordered business email from Nisc Cloud.

---

## Before you create mailboxes

1. Your **email plan** is active under **Emails** in the dashboard.
2. Your **domain** points mail to Nisc Cloud via [DNS MX records](/docs/email/dns-records) — some setups configure this automatically.

---

## Create a mailbox

1. Sign in at [npanel.nisccloud.com](https://npanel.nisccloud.com).
2. Click **Emails** in the sidebar.
3. Click your **email plan** (often named after your domain).
4. Look for **Create mailbox**, **Add account**, or **Mailboxes**.
5. Enter:
   - **Username** — part before `@` (e.g. `info`, `sales`, `yourname`)
   - **Password** — strong password (save it securely)
   - **Display name** — how recipients see you (optional)
   - **Storage quota** — if plan allows per-user limits
6. Click **Create** or **Save**.
7. Full address is `username@yourdomain.com`.

Repeat for each employee or role (`support@`, `accounts@`, etc.) within your planNisc Cloud's mailbox limit.

---

## Reset a mailbox password

1. Open **Emails → [your plan] → Mailboxes**.
2. Select the user → **Change password** or **Reset**.
3. Enter new password twice and save.
4. Update password on phones and Outlook if already configured.

---

## Forwarding and aliases (if available)

Some plans support **forwarders** (send copy to Gmail) or **aliases** (multiple names, one inbox):

1. On the email plan page, open **Forwarders** or **Aliases**.
2. Add source address and destination.
3. Save.

Exact options depend on your product tier.

---

## Test sending and receiving

1. Configure [DNS](/docs/email/dns-records) if not done.
2. Open [Webmail](/docs/email/webmail) and log in as the new user.
3. Send a test email to a personal Gmail address.
4. Reply from Gmail to confirm inbound mail works.

If mail fails, wait for DNS propagation (up to 24–48 hours) and verify MX records.
