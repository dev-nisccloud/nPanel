**Business email** gives you addresses like `you@yourcompany.co.ke` instead of a free Gmail or Yahoo account. Mail is hosted on Nisc Cloud and managed from the **Emails** menu in the dashboard.

---

## Order business email

1. Visit [nisccloud.com](https://www.nisccloud.com) → **Email** or **Business email**.
2. Choose plan by number of mailboxes and storage.
3. Select the **domain** email will use (register domain in same order if needed).
4. Checkout and pay.

After provisioning, open **Emails** in the dashboard.

---

## Open email management

1. Sign in at [npanel.nisccloud.com](https://npanel.nisccloud.com).
2. Click **Emails** under **Cloud Services**.
3. Click your **email plan** or domain.
4. Create mailboxes, view server settings, and open webmail links.

---

## Setup flow (summary)

1. [Create mailboxes](/docs/email/setup)  usernames and passwords.
2. [Configure DNS](/docs/email/dns-records)  MX, SPF, DKIM on your domain.
3. [Use webmail](/docs/email/webmail) in the browser.
4. [Connect phone or Outlook](/docs/email/clients) with IMAP/SMTP.

Email may take a few hours to work worldwide after DNS changes.

---

## Email vs hosting mail tab

| Location | When to use |
|----------|-------------|
| **Emails** menu | Standalone business email product |
| **Hosting → Mail** tab | Mailboxes bundled with shared hosting plan |

Use the screen that matches what you purchased.

## Billing

Email renews with your subscription. Pay **Billing → Invoices** before expiry or sending/receiving may stop.
