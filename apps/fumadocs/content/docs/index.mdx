Welcome to the Nisc Cloud documentation. These guides are written for **customers** who use the Nisc Cloud website and the **customer dashboard** at [npanel.nisccloud.com](https://npanel.nisccloud.com).

Each guide tells you exactly where to click in the left sidebar, what you should see on screen, and what to do next. We use plain English and walk through tasks one step at a time.

## What Nisc Cloud offers

From [nisccloud.com](https://www.nisccloud.com) you can order and manage:

| Category | What you can do |
|----------|-----------------|
| **Domains** | Register new names, transfer domains in, manage DNS and nameservers |
| **Hosting** | Shared hosting, WordPress hosting, VPS, VDS, and dedicated servers |
| **Business email** | Professional mailboxes on your domain |
| **SSL** | Secure your website with HTTPS certificates |
| **Digital marketing** | Email campaigns, SEO reports, paid ads, and creative assets |
| **AI tools** | Zeepo AI Agent for content and marketing help |
| **Billing** | View invoices, manage subscriptions, and pay with M-Pesa or card |

Everything you order appears in your dashboard so you can manage it in one place.
