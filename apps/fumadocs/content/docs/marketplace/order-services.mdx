---

This guide walks through **ordering a new service** from **Marketplace** inside the customer dashboard.

---

## Step 1: Open Marketplace

1. Sign in at [npanel.nisccloud.com](https://npanel.nisccloud.com).
2. Confirm you are on the **correct organization** (top bar or organization name) — the order bills to this account.
3. Click **More Services → Marketplace**.

---

## Step 2: Choose a product

1. Browse categories or use **Search**.
2. Click a product card (e.g. “Business Email”, “VPS Starter”, “Domain .co.ke”).
3. Read plan features on the product page.
4. Select options:
   - **Billing cycle**  monthly, annual (often discounted)
   - **Domain**  register new, transfer, or use existing from **My Domains**
   - **Quantity**  mailboxes, server size, etc.
5. Click **Add to cart** or **Order now**.

---

## Step 3: Review cart

1. Open **Cart** icon or **View cart** link.
2. Check each line item  name, term, price.
3. Remove mistakes with **Remove** or change quantity.
4. Apply **coupon code** if you have one.
5. Click **Checkout** or **Proceed to payment**.

---

## Step 4: Billing details

1. Confirm **billing name and address**  edit via **Account Settings → Settings → Billing** if wrong.
2. Confirm **email** for receipt on [My Profile](/docs/npanel/profile).

---

## Step 5: Pay

1. Select **M-Pesa** or **card**.
2. **M-Pesa:** enter phone number → approve STK push → wait for success message.
3. **Card:** complete secure payment form.
4. Do not close the window until confirmation appears.

Details: [Checkout and payment](/docs/billing/checkout).

---

## Step 6: Confirm provisioning

1. Go to **Billing → Invoices**  new invoice should show **Paid**.
2. Open the relevant menu:
   - Domain → **Domains → My Domains**
   - Hosting → **Hosting**
   - Email → **Emails**
   - VPS → **Cloud Servers → VPS**
3. If service does not appear within 30–60 minutes, contact support with invoice number.

---

## Upgrade or add to existing service

Some products **upgrade** current plans instead of creating new rows:

- Read product page for “upgrade” vs “additional instance”
- When unsure, contact support before paying to avoid duplicate hosting accounts
