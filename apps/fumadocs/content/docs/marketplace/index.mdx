The **Marketplace** lets you order more Nisc Cloud products **without leaving the dashboard** — hosting upgrades, domains, email, marketing packages, and other add-ons appear in your account after checkout.

---

## Open Marketplace

1. Sign in at [npanel.nisccloud.com](https://npanel.nisccloud.com).
2. Scroll the left sidebar to **More Services**.
3. Click **Marketplace**.
4. Browse categories, featured products, and plans.

You can also shop on [www.nisccloud.com](https://www.nisccloud.com) — orders attach to the same account when you use the same login email.

---

## What you can order

Typical Marketplace categories mirror the public website:

- **Domains** — register or transfer
- **Hosting** — shared, WordPress, VPS, VDS, dedicated
- **Email** — business mailboxes
- **SSL** and security add-ons
- **Digital marketing** — SEO, ads, email marketing packages
- **Design and creative services**
- **Bundles** — Launchpad, Orbit, or combined domain + hosting offers (when listed)

Product availability changes over time — use search or filters on the Marketplace page.

---

## How ordering works

1. Click a product → choose options (plan tier, domain name, term).
2. **Add to cart**.
3. Open **Cart** or **Checkout**.
4. Confirm billing details (from [Settings → Billing](/docs/npanel/settings)).
5. Pay with M-Pesa or card — see [Checkout](/docs/billing/checkout).
6. New service appears under the correct sidebar menu (**Hosting**, **Domains**, etc.) after provisioning.

Orders apply to the **team you are logged into** — switch teams before checkout if you manage multiple companies.
