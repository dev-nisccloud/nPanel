**Zeepo AI Agent** is Nisc CloudNisc Cloud's conversational AI assistant inside your dashboard. You ask questions in plain English and get drafts, suggestions, and step-by-step help for marketing and content tasks.

---

## Open Zeepo AI Agent

1. Sign in at [npanel.nisccloud.com](https://npanel.nisccloud.com).
2. In the left sidebar, find **Gen AI Tools**.
3. Click **Zeepo AI Agent**.
4. The chat interface opens in the main content area.

---

## Start a conversation

1. Click the message box at the bottom (or **New chat** if continuing old threads).
2. Type what you need, for example:
   - “Write a welcome email for new customers of my online shop.”
   - “Give me five Facebook post ideas for a Nairobi restaurant.”
   - “Explain how to add an MX record for my domain in simple steps.”
3. Press **Enter** or click **Send**.
4. Read the response. Ask follow-up questions to refine tone, length, or language (English, Swahili mix, etc.).

---

## Good uses for Zeepo

| Task | Example prompt |
|------|----------------|
| Marketing copy | “Draft a 200-word promo for .co.ke domain sale.” |
| Social captions | “Instagram caption for hosting launch with call to action.” |
| Email subjects | “10 subject lines for end-of-year newsletter.” |
| Blog outlines | “Outline for blog post on why business email beats Gmail.” |
| How-to summaries | “Steps to explain SSL to a non-technical client.” |

Zeepo helps draft content  you should edit and fact-check before publishing.

---

## Chat history and new sessions

- **Previous chats** may appear in a sidebar list  click to reopen.
- **New chat** clears context for a unrelated topic.
- Do not rely on Zeepo to remember account-specific passwords or invoice numbers.

For account tasks (pay invoice, change DNS), use the dedicated docs and dashboard menus  Zeepo is guidance only.

---

## Zeepo vs AI Email Builder

| | Zeepo AI Agent | AI Email Builder |
|---|----------------|------------------|
| Location | Gen AI Tools | Email Marketing |
| Best for | General text, ideas, Q&A | Visual email templates for campaigns |
| Output | Chat messages | Email HTML/layout |

See [Email marketing](/docs/marketing/email-marketing) for campaign sending after design.

---

## Tips for better answers

1. **Be specific**  industry, audience, city, product name.
2. **Set tone**  formal, friendly, youthful.
3. **Set length**  “short SMS style” vs “800-word article”.
4. **Iterate**  “Make it shorter”, “Add M-Pesa payment mention”, “Translate key paragraph to Swahili.”
