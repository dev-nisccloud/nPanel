Nisc Cloud includes **AI-powered tools** to help you write content, plan marketing, and work faster inside the dashboard  without leaving [npanel.nisccloud.com](https://npanel.nisccloud.com).

---

## What is available

| Tool | Menu location |
|------|----------------|
| **Zeepo AI Agent** | **Gen AI Tools → Zeepo AI Agent** |
| **AI Email Builder** | **Digital Marketing → Email Marketing → AI Email Builder** |

Zeepo is a general assistant; AI Email Builder focuses on newsletter and campaign design.

## Privacy and accuracy

- Do not paste passwords, M-Pesa PINs, or confidential customer data into AI chats.
- Review AI-generated text for facts, tone, and legal compliance before publishing or sending to customers.
- AI features may require an active plan or usage limits  check **Billing → Subscriptions**.
