**Email marketing** in the dashboard lets you send newsletters and promotional emails to your contact lists — separate from day-to-day **business email** mailboxes under **Emails**.

Access it under **Digital Marketing → Email Marketing** in the sidebar.

---

## Open email marketing

1. Sign in at [npanel.nisccloud.com](https://npanel.nisccloud.com).
2. In the left sidebar, click **Email Marketing** (under **Digital Marketing**).
3. The section expands to show sub-menus:

| Sub-menu | Purpose |
|----------|---------|
| **Campaigns** | Create and send email blasts |
| **Audiences** | Contact lists and segments |
| **AI Email Builder** | Design emails with AI help |
| **Domains** | Verify domain for sending |

---

## Verify sending domain

Before recipients see mail from `@yourdomain.com`, verify the domain:

1. Click **Email Marketing → Domains**.
2. Click **Add domain** and enter your domain name.
3. Copy **DNS TXT/CNAME records** shown on screen.
4. Add records at **Domains → My Domains → [domain] → DNS** (or your DNS provider).
5. Return to **Domains** in email marketing and click **Verify**.
6. Wait for DNS propagation if verification fails at first.

See also [Email DNS records](/docs/email/dns-records) for SPF/DKIM concepts.

---

## Build an audience

1. Click **Email Marketing → Audiences**.
2. Click **Create audience** or **Import contacts**.
3. Upload CSV with email, name columns OR add contacts manually.
4. Tag or segment lists (customers, newsletter, etc.) if the tool supports segments.
5. Save audience.

Only email people who opted in — follow Kenya Data Protection Act and anti-spam best practices.

---

## Create a campaign

1. Click **Email Marketing → Campaigns**.
2. Click **New campaign** or **Create**.
3. Choose audience/list.
4. Write subject line and preview text.
5. Design body using editor or import from **AI Email Builder**.
6. Send **test email** to yourself.
7. Schedule send or **Send now**.
8. Review opens/clicks on campaign report after send.

---

## Use AI Email Builder

1. Click **Email Marketing → AI Email Builder** (or **AI Email Builder** under sub-menu — may open dedicated page).
2. Describe the email you want (promotion, welcome series, event invite).
3. Review generated layout and copy — edit before sending.
4. Save template and use in a **Campaign**.

AI output should be reviewed for accuracy and brand tone before sending to customers.
