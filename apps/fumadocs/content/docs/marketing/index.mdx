Nisc Cloud **digital marketing tools** help you grow online — send email campaigns, review SEO performance, manage ad campaigns, and access design assets. All are under **Digital Marketing** in the left sidebar at [npanel.nisccloud.com](https://npanel.nisccloud.com).

You can also learn about services on [nisccloud.com](https://www.nisccloud.com) under digital marketing pages before ordering.

---

## What is available in the dashboard

| Menu | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| **Media Buying** → Meta / Google / LinkedIn Ads | Paid advertising campaigns |
| **Email Marketing** | Campaigns, audiences, AI email builder, sending domains |
| **SEO → SEO Reports** | Search engine optimization reports for your sites |
| **Creatives & Design** | Creative files and design deliverables |

Exact features depend on what you purchased or what is included in your plan.

---

## Order marketing services

1. Browse offerings on [nisccloud.com](https://www.nisccloud.com) (SEO, social media, email marketing, etc.).
2. Or open **More Services → Marketplace** in the dashboard.
3. Checkout and pay.
4. New tools appear under **Digital Marketing** after activation.

## Billing

Marketing products may bill monthly or per project. Check **Billing → Subscriptions** and **Invoices** for renewals and one-time fees.
