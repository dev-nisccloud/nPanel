---
title: Creative assets
description: Access design files, brand creatives, and marketing deliverables in the customer dashboard.
---

**Creatives & Design** is where you **download and review creative work**  logos, social graphics, ad banners, brochures, and other design deliverables produced for your Nisc Cloud marketing services.

---

## Open Creatives & Design

1. Sign in at [npanel.nisccloud.com](https://npanel.nisccloud.com).
2. Under **Digital Marketing**, click **Creatives & Design**.
3. The main page lists projects, folders, or asset collections assigned to your account.

If the page is empty, you may not have ordered design services yet  browse **Brand identity**, **Web design**, or **Creatives** on [nisccloud.com](https://www.nisccloud.com) or **Marketplace**.

---

## Browse and download files

1. Click a **project name** or **folder** (e.g. “Logo pack”, “Meta ad set Q1”).
2. Thumbnails or file list show available formats (PNG, JPG, PDF, SVG, MP4).
3. Click **Download** on individual files or **Download all** for a ZIP.
4. Save to your computer and share with your team or upload to ad platforms.

---

## Upload briefs or feedback (if available)

Some workflows let you submit requests from the dashboard:

1. Click **New request**, **Upload brief**, or **Add comment**.
2. Describe what you need (dimensions, platform, message, brand colors).
3. Attach reference images or old logo.
4. Submit  designers may reply with new versions in same folder.

Check notifications or email for “asset ready” messages.

---

## Use assets in other dashboard tools

| Asset type | Use in |
|------------|--------|
| Ad banners | **Media Buying → Meta/Google/LinkedIn Ads** |
| Email headers | **Email Marketing → Campaigns** or **AI Email Builder** |
| Logo | Website via [File Manager](/docs/hosting/manage-hosting) or WordPress media library |

Always use the **latest version** from Creatives & Design to keep brand consistent.

---

## File permissions

Assets are visible only to members of your **organization**. Invite colleagues under [Settings → Invitations](/docs/npanel/settings) if they need download access.
