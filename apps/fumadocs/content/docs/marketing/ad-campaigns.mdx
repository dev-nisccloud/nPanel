---
title: Ad campaigns
description: Manage Meta, Google, and LinkedIn advertising from Media Buying in the customer dashboard.
---

**Media Buying** in the dashboard is where you work with **paid advertising** on Meta (Facebook/Instagram), Google, and LinkedIn. Campaign setup may be self-service, assisted, or managed depending on what you ordered from Nisc Cloud.

---

## Open Media Buying

1. Sign in at [npanel.nisccloud.com](https://npanel.nisccloud.com).
2. Under **Digital Marketing**, click **Media Buying**.
3. Sub-menus appear:

| Sub-menu | Platform |
|----------|----------|
| **Meta Ads** | Facebook and Instagram |
| **Google Ads** | Search, Display, YouTube (plan dependent) |
| **LinkedIn Ads** | LinkedIn sponsored content and lead gen |

Click the platform you use.

---

## Meta Ads

1. Click **Media Buying → Meta Ads**.
2. You may see:
   - List of active and past campaigns
   - Spend and performance summary (impressions, clicks, conversions)
   - Links to create or request new campaigns
3. To start new work, click **Create campaign**, **New request**, or follow on-screen brief form:
   - Business goal (leads, traffic, sales)
   - Target audience (location, age, interests)
   - Budget and duration
   - Creative assets (images, video, copy)
4. Submit  Nisc Cloud team may build campaigns if you purchased managed ads.

Connect your **Meta Business Manager** account when prompted for direct ad account linking.

---

## Google Ads

1. Click **Media Buying → Google Ads**.
2. Review dashboard for campaigns, keywords, and spend.
3. Create or request campaigns with:
   - Target keywords or products
   - Landing page URL on your Nisc Cloud-hosted site
   - Daily/monthly budget
4. Ensure landing pages load fast and use [SSL](/docs/ssl).

Link **Google Ads** account when integration step appears.

---

## LinkedIn Ads

1. Click **Media Buying → LinkedIn Ads**.
2. View B2B campaign performance  leads, clicks, impressions.
3. Request campaigns targeting job titles, industries, or company sizes in Kenya and abroad.
4. Provide professional copy and logo from [Creative assets](/docs/marketing/creative-assets) if available.

---

## Track results

Check each platform page weekly for:

- **Spend vs budget**
- **Cost per click (CPC)** or **cost per lead (CPL)**
- **Conversion tracking**  confirm pixel or Google tag fires on thank-you page

Ask support to install Meta Pixel or Google Tag on your WordPress site if not already done.

---

## Billing for ads

**Two costs:**

1. **Nisc Cloud service fee**  on **Billing → Invoices**
2. **Ad platform spend**  billed by Meta/Google/LinkedIn or through prepaid wallet depending on agreement

Clarify billing model when ordering from [nisccloud.com](https://www.nisccloud.com).
