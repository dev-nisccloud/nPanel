**SEO reports** show how your website performs in search engines  visibility, keywords, technical issues, and recommendations. Nisc Cloud provides these under **Digital Marketing → SEO → SEO Reports**.

Order SEO services on [nisccloud.com](https://www.nisccloud.com) if reports are not yet on your account.

---

## Open SEO reports

1. Sign in at [npanel.nisccloud.com](https://npanel.nisccloud.com).
2. In the left sidebar, under **Digital Marketing**, click **SEO**.
3. Click **SEO Reports** in the sub-menu.
4. You see a list of reports or projects tied to your domain(s).

---

## View a report

1. On **SEO Reports** page, click a **report name**, **domain**, or **View** link.
2. The report detail page may include sections such as:
   - **Overview score**  summary health of SEO
   - **Keywords**  terms you rank for or could target
   - **On-page issues**  missing titles, slow pages, broken links
   - **Technical SEO**  mobile usability, indexability, HTTPS
   - **Backlinks**  sites linking to you (if included in plan)
   - **Recommendations**  prioritized actions to improve rankings

Read each section top to bottom. Fix critical errors first (404 pages, no HTTPS, blocked indexing).

---

## Connect a new website to reporting

If you can add sites yourself:

1. On **SEO Reports**, click **Add website**, **Add domain**, or **New report**.
2. Enter full URL (`https://yourdomain.com`).
3. Confirm ownership if asked (DNS TXT record or meta tag  similar to domain verification).
4. Wait for first crawl  may take 24–48 hours.

If you do not see an add button, your plan may be managed by Nisc Cloud  contact support to link your domain.

---

## Act on recommendations

Typical fixes you or your developer can do:

| Recommendation | Where to fix |
|----------------|--------------|
| Missing meta title/description | WordPress SEO plugin or page editor |
| Slow page speed | Optimize images, caching on [hosting](/docs/hosting/manage-hosting) |
| No SSL | [Enable SSL](/docs/ssl/enable) |
| Mobile issues | Responsive theme update |
| Broken links | Fix in WordPress or site builder |

Re-run or wait for next scheduled report to see score improvements.

---

## Export or share

Look for **Download PDF**, **Export**, or **Share link** on report page to send to your team or accountant. Options vary by plan.
