**Settings** (under **Account Settings** in the sidebar) is where you manage your **company or team account**  the organization that owns your domains, hosting, and invoices. This is different from **My Profile**, which is only your personal login details.

---

## Open Settings

1. Sign in at [npanel.nisccloud.com](https://npanel.nisccloud.com).
2. In the left sidebar, under **Account Settings**, click **Settings**.
3. The settings page opens, usually with tabs at the top.

Common tabs:

| Tab | Purpose |
|-----|---------|
| **Billing** | Company billing name, address, and tax details used on invoices |
| **Members** | People who can access this teamNisc Cloud's dashboard |
| **Invitations** | Pending invites you sent to new members |

Your screen may show slightly different labels, but the ideas are the same.

---

## Update company billing details

Correct billing details help invoices and receipts show the right legal name and address.

1. Open **Account Settings → Settings**.
2. Click the **Billing** tab.
3. Fill in or update:
   - Company or individual name
   - Address, city, postal code, country
   - Phone number
   - Tax or VAT number (if applicable)
4. Click **Save** or **Update**.
5. New invoices should use the updated information; old invoices keep what was printed when they were issued.

For paying invoices and viewing subscription renewals, see [Billing overview](/docs/billing).

---

## View team members

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Click the **Members** tab.
3. You see a list of people with access to this team, often with their email and role.

Members can manage services according to their permissions. If someone should no longer have access, an owner or admin can remove them (see below).

---

## Invite a new member

Use this when a colleague needs access to the same domains, hosting, and billing.

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Click the **Invitations** tab (or look for **Invite member** on the Members tab).
3. Click **Invite**, **Add member**, or similar.
4. Enter the personNisc Cloud's **email address**.
5. Choose a **role** if asked (for example admin vs member  exact roles depend on your account).
6. Send the invitation.
7. The recipient receives an email with a link to accept and create or link their login.
8. After they accept, they appear under **Members** and can sign in and select this team.

Tell invitees to use [npanel.nisccloud.com](https://npanel.nisccloud.com) and pick your company name after login.

---

## Remove a member

1. Open **Settings → Members**.
2. Find the person in the list.
3. Click **Remove**, **Delete**, or the menu (⋯) next to their name.
4. Confirm removal.

Removed users can no longer open this teamNisc Cloud's dashboard. They keep their personal login if they had one, but lose access to this companyNisc Cloud's services.

---

## Switch between teams

If you belong to multiple companies:

1. Sign out, or use the team switcher in the top bar if available.
2. Sign in again and select the other company name.
3. Sidebar lists and invoices change to that teamNisc Cloud's data.
