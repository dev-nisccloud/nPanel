The **customer dashboard** (also called nPanel) is the website at [npanel.nisccloud.com](https://npanel.nisccloud.com). It is **only for customers**  the place where you manage domains, hosting, email, billing, and marketing tools you have purchased from Nisc Cloud.

You do not use the dashboard to browse marketing pages or buy new products (that happens on [nisccloud.com](https://www.nisccloud.com)), but after you buy, **everything you own lives here**.

## What the dashboard is for

Use the dashboard when you want to:

- See all your domains, hosting accounts, email plans, and servers in one list
- Change DNS records, nameservers, or mail settings
- Upload website files, create databases, or install WordPress
- Pay invoices and check subscription renewal dates
- Run email campaigns, view SEO reports, or use Zeepo AI
- Update your profile or invite teammates to your company account

## How to open it

1. Go to [https://npanel.nisccloud.com](https://npanel.nisccloud.com).
2. Enter your email and password.
3. Select your company or team if asked.

Detailed sign-in steps are in [Sign in and access](/docs/npanel/sign-in).

## Main parts of the screen

When you are logged in, you typically see:

**Top bar**

- Your company or account name
- Quick actions such as switching teams or signing out (exact items depend on your setup)

**Left sidebar**

- All menus grouped by category (Cloud Services, Digital Marketing, Billing, and so on)
- Click a menu item to open that section; some items expand to show sub-menus

**Main content area**

- The page for whatever you clicked  for example a list of domains, hosting tabs, or an invoice
