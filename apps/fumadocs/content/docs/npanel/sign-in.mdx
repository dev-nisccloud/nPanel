title: Sign in and access
description: How to log in to the customer dashboard, choose your organization, and recover access if you are locked out.
---

This guide walks you through signing in to [npanel.nisccloud.com](https://npanel.nisccloud.com) and reaching the correct account every time.

---

## Sign in with email and password

1. Open your browser and go to [https://npanel.nisccloud.com](https://npanel.nisccloud.com).
2. You should see the **Sign in** page.
3. Enter the **email address** you used when you registered or checked out on Nisc Cloud.
4. Enter your **password**.
5. Click **Sign in** (or **Log in**).

If your details are correct, you are taken into the dashboard. If you belong to more than one organization, continue to the next section.

---

## Choose the right organization

Some users are members of more than one organization (for example their own business and a client account).

1. After sign-in, if you see an **organization picker** or list of companies, read each name carefully.
2. Click the organization whose services you want to manage.
3. You land on **Home** for that organization.

Every domain, hosting package, and invoice belongs to **one organization**. If you do not see a service you expect, sign out and sign back in, then pick a different organization  or ask your account owner to invite you to the correct organization. See [Settings and organization](/docs/npanel/settings).

---

## First time after purchasing on the website

If you just completed checkout on [nisccloud.com](https://www.nisccloud.com):

1. Use the **same email** you entered at checkout to sign in.
2. If you created a password during checkout, use that password.
3. If the site emailed you a **set password** or **welcome** link, open it first, choose a password, then sign in at npanel.nisccloud.com.
4. Open **Billing → Invoices** to confirm your payment recorded.
5. Open the relevant menu (**Domains**, **Hosting**, **Emails**, etc.) to see provisioned services. Provisioning may take a few minutes.

---

## Forgot your password

1. On the sign-in page, click **Forgot password** (or similar wording).
2. Enter your account email.
3. Check your inbox (and spam folder) for a reset link from Nisc Cloud.
4. Open the link, enter a new password twice, and save.
5. Return to [npanel.nisccloud.com](https://npanel.nisccloud.com) and sign in with the new password.

If you never receive the email, confirm you typed the correct address or contact support via [nisccloud.com/contact](https://www.nisccloud.com/contact).

---

## Sign out safely

When you finish on a shared or public computer:

1. Look at the top of the dashboard for your profile menu or **Sign out**.
2. Click **Sign out**.
3. Close the browser tab.

---

## Browser and security tips

- Use an up-to-date browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge).
- Bookmark [https://npanel.nisccloud.com](https://npanel.nisccloud.com) and avoid login links from unknown emails.
- Use a unique password for your Nisc Cloud account.
- Keep the email on your [My Profile](/docs/npanel/profile) page current so you receive invoices and reset links.
