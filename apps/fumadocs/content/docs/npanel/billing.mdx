## Invoices

Open **Billing** → **Invoices** to download PDF invoices and see payment status.

## Payment methods

Add or update cards and other supported methods under **Billing** → **Payment methods**. Failed payments may suspend services after grace periods.

## Renewals

Most services renew automatically when a valid payment method is on file. You can change plan or cancel from the service’s management page (subject to product terms).
