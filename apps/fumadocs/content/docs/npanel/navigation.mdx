The **left sidebar** is the main way to move around the customer dashboard. Menus are grouped into sections. Some items expand to show sub-menus when you click them.

This page lists **exact menu labels** as they appear in the dashboard so you can follow along while logged in at [npanel.nisccloud.com](https://npanel.nisccloud.com).

---

## Cloud Services

### Home

- **Menu:** Cloud Services → **Home**
- **Use it for:** Account summary and quick overview after login.
- **Guide:** [Dashboard home](/docs/npanel/home)

### Domains

Click **Domains** to expand three sub-items:

| Sub-menu | Use it for |
|----------|------------|
| **Get a Domain** | Search and register a new domain name |
| **Transfer** | Move a domain from another registrar to Nisc Cloud |
| **My Domains** | List all domains you own; open one for DNS and settings |

**Guides:** [Register a domain](/docs/domains/register) · [Transfer](/docs/domains/transfer) · [Domain portfolio](/docs/domains/portfolio)

### Hosting

- **Menu:** Cloud Services → **Hosting**
- **Use it for:** List of shared hosting and website hosting packages. Click one to manage files, DNS, mail, databases, WordPress, and backups.
- **Guide:** [Manage hosting](/docs/hosting/manage-hosting)

### WordPress

- **Menu:** Cloud Services → **WordPress**
- **Use it for:** WordPress sites linked to your account  open a site for WordPress-specific management.
- **Guide:** [WordPress overview](/docs/wordpress)

### Emails

- **Menu:** Cloud Services → **Emails**
- **Use it for:** Business email plans and mailboxes on your domain.
- **Guide:** [Business email](/docs/email)

### Cloud Servers

Click **Cloud Servers** to expand:

| Sub-menu | Use it for |
|----------|------------|
| **VPS** | Virtual private servers  root access, scalable compute |
| **VDS** | Virtual dedicated servers  stronger isolation and resources |
| **Dedicated** | Physical dedicated servers for maximum performance |

**Guides:** [VPS](/docs/hosting/vps) · [VDS](/docs/hosting/vds) · [Dedicated](/docs/hosting/dedicated)

## Tips for finding anything quickly

1. **Start from the group name**  domains live under Cloud Services; invoices under Billing.
2. **Lists first, details second**  open **My Domains** or **Hosting**, then click one item for tabs like DNS or File Manager.
3. **If a menu is missing**  your plan may not include that product yet. Use **Marketplace** or [nisccloud.com](https://www.nisccloud.com) to order it.
