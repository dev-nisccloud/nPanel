After you sign in to [npanel.nisccloud.com](https://npanel.nisccloud.com), the first page is usually **Home** under **Cloud Services** in the left sidebar.

---

## Open the Home screen

1. Sign in to the dashboard.
2. In the left sidebar, under **Cloud Services**, click **Home**.
3. If you are already on another page, clicking **Home** always returns you to this starting point.

The URL in your browser will look like `https://npanel.nisccloud.com/your-team-name/dashboard` (your team name appears in the path).

---

## What Home is for

Home gives you a **summary** of your account. Think of it as a front door: you can see what is active, spot items that need attention, and jump quickly into deeper pages like a specific domain or hosting package.

Exact widgets and numbers depend on your plan, but Home commonly helps you:

- See how many domains, hosting accounts, or email plans you have
- Notice overdue invoices or upcoming renewals (if shown)
- Open recent or pinned services faster than browsing long lists

If you need full detail or to change settings, use the sidebar — **Domains**, **Hosting**, **Emails**, **Billing**, and so on.

---

## Move from Home to a specific service

**Open a domain**

1. Click **Domains** in the sidebar.
2. Click **My Domains**.
3. Click the domain name you want.
4. You see tabs such as **Overview**, **DNS**, **Nameservers**, and **Protect Brand**.

**Open hosting**

1. Click **Hosting** in the sidebar.
2. Click your hosting account or website name in the list.
3. Use tabs like **Overview**, **File Manager**, **Databases**, **WordPress**, and **Backup**.

**Open email**

1. Click **Emails** in the sidebar.
2. Click your email plan.
3. Create mailboxes, open webmail links, or view DNS requirements.

**Check billing**

1. Under **Billing**, click **Invoices** or **Subscriptions**.
2. Open an invoice to pay or download, or open a subscription to see renewal date and price.

---

## When Home looks empty

If Home or your service lists are empty right after purchase:

1. Wait a few minutes and refresh the page — provisioning can take time.
2. Confirm you selected the **correct team** at login.
3. Open **Billing → Invoices** to verify payment completed.
4. If payment succeeded but services still do not appear after an hour, contact support with your invoice number.
