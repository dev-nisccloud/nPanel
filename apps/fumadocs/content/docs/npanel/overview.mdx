## Main areas

| Area | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| **Dashboard** | Snapshot of services and alerts |
| **Services** | Hosting, WordPress, VPS, email, SSL |
| **Domains** | Registration, transfer, DNS |
| **Billing** | Invoices, payment methods, renewals |
| **Support** | Tickets and knowledge base |
| **Account** | Profile, security, team access |

## Tips

- Use search (where available) to jump to a service quickly
- Enable 2FA on your account for better security
- Keep billing contacts up to date to avoid missed renewals
