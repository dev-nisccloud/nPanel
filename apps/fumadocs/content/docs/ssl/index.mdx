An **SSL certificate** enables **HTTPS**  the padlock in the browser  so visitors know their connection to your site is encrypted. Search engines also favor HTTPS sites.

Nisc Cloud offers SSL for domains and hosting you manage in the dashboard.

---

## When you need SSL

- Any website collecting passwords, forms, or payments
- Business credibility (browsers warn on HTTP sites)
- Email and webmail hostnames
- SEO and modern browser requirements

---

## Types of SSL

| Type | Use case |
|------|----------|
| **Free / LetNisc Cloud's Encrypt** | Most websites on Nisc Cloud hosting  auto or one-click |
| **Standard DV** | Quick validation for one domain |
| **Wildcard / multi-domain** | Many subdomains or multiple names (plan dependent) |

Order paid SSL on [nisccloud.com](https://www.nisccloud.com) if required; free options may be included with hosting.

---

## Enable SSL on hosting

For **shared** or **WordPress hosting**:

1. Follow [Enable SSL on hosting](/docs/ssl/enable).
2. Force HTTPS in WordPress or `.htaccess` after certificate is active.

For **VPS, VDS, dedicated**:

1. Follow [Install SSL on your server](/docs/ssl/install) with Certbot or purchased cert.

## Troubleshooting HTTPS

- **Certificate mismatch**  cert must match domain name in browser URL
- **Mixed content**  site loads HTTP images/scripts; update URLs to HTTPS
- **Not secure warning**  certificate expired; renew under SSL or hosting tools

After enabling SSL, wait a few minutes and hard-refresh browser (Ctrl+F5).
