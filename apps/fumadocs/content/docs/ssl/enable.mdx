---
title: Enable SSL
description: Turn on HTTPS for your domain.
---

## Steps

1. Ensure your domain’s **A** / **CNAME** records point to your hosting.
2. In nPanel, open the hosting or SSL service for that domain.
3. Choose **Enable SSL** / **Let’s Encrypt** (or install a purchased certificate).
4. Wait for issuance, then visit `https://yourdomain.com`.

## Force HTTPS

After SSL is active, enable “Force HTTPS” or add a redirect from HTTP to HTTPS so all traffic uses the secure URL.

## Troubleshooting

- **Validation failed:** DNS not pointing correctly yet
- **Mixed content:** update hardcoded `http://` assets in your site
- **Renewal:** free certificates renew automatically when DNS stays correct
