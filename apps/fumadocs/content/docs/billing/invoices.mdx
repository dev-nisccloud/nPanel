title: Invoices

---

An **invoice** is a bill for a specific charge  your first hosting order, a domain renewal, or a one-time add-on. Every paid or unpaid bill appears under **Billing → Invoices**.

---

## Open the invoices list

1. Sign in at [npanel.nisccloud.com](https://npanel.nisccloud.com).
2. In the left sidebar, click **Billing**.
3. Click **Invoices**.
4. You see a table or list of invoices, usually with:
   - Invoice number
   - Date
   - Amount
   - Status (Paid, Unpaid, Overdue, Cancelled)

Use filters or search if available to find a specific invoice number from an email.

---

## Open one invoice

1. On the invoices list, click the invoice **number** or **View** link.
2. The invoice detail page shows:
   - Bill-to name and address
   - Line items (each product or renewal)
   - Subtotal, tax if any, and **total due**
   - Payment status and payment date (if already paid)

---

## Pay an unpaid invoice

1. Open an invoice with status **Unpaid** or **Due**.
2. Click **Pay now**, **Pay invoice**, or **Complete payment**.
3. Select payment method:
   - **M-Pesa**  approve STK push on your phone
   - **Card**  enter card details on the secure payment form
4. Wait for confirmation on screen.
5. Refresh the invoice  status should change to **Paid**.
6. Your services remain active or are restored after successful payment.

Pay before the due date on renewal invoices to avoid domain or hosting suspension.

---

## Download or print an invoice

1. Open a **Paid** invoice (or any invoice you need for records).
2. Look for **Download PDF**, **Print**, or a download icon.
3. Save the file for your accounting or tax records.

If no download button appears, use your browserNisc Cloud's print function on the invoice page or contact support for a copy.

---

## Understand invoice line items

Each row on an invoice typically matches one product:

| Line item example | Meaning |
|-------------------|---------|
| Domain registration / renewal | `.co.ke`, `.com`, or other TLD for one year |
| Shared hosting | Website hosting plan for a term |
| Business email | Mailbox plan or seat count |
| VPS / VDS / Dedicated | Cloud server billing period |
| SSL | Certificate issue or renewal |

If a line item looks wrong, note the invoice number and contact support before paying.

---

## Invoices vs subscriptions

- **Invoice** = a bill for a point in time (you pay this document).
- **Subscription** = the ongoing service record with a next renewal date.

A renewal often creates a **new invoice** linked to an existing **subscription**. See [Subscriptions](/docs/billing/subscriptions).
