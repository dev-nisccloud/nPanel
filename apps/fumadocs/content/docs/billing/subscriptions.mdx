---
title: Subscriptions
description: View active subscriptions, renewal dates, and what happens when a subscription renews or expires.
---

A **subscription** is a recurring Nisc Cloud service  hosting renewed yearly, a monthly server, or similar. Subscriptions do not replace invoices; when a renewal is due, the system usually creates an **invoice** you pay.

Manage subscriptions under **Billing → Subscriptions** in the dashboard.

---

## Open the subscriptions list

1. Sign in at [npanel.nisccloud.com](https://npanel.nisccloud.com).
2. In the left sidebar, click **Billing**.
3. Click **Subscriptions**.
4. You see active (and sometimes cancelled) subscriptions with:
   - Product name (hosting plan, domain, email, etc.)
   - Billing cycle (monthly, annually)
   - **Next renewal date** or **Expiry date**
   - Status (Active, Suspended, Cancelled)

---

## Open one subscription

1. Click a subscription row or **View** link.
2. The detail page may show:
   - Linked service (which domain or hosting account)
   - Price at renewal
   - Start date and next billing date
   - Related invoices

Use this page to know **when** you will be billed again, not necessarily to change technical DNS or hosting settings  those stay under **Domains**, **Hosting**, or **Emails**.

---

## How renewal works

1. Before the renewal date, Nisc Cloud may email the billing contact on your account.
2. A new **invoice** appears under **Billing → Invoices**, often a few days before or on the renewal date.
3. You pay the invoice with M-Pesa or card (same as any other bill).
4. When paid, the subscription **next renewal date** moves forward and the service continues.

If auto-pay is not enabled, you must pay manually each cycle. Check each renewal invoice in **Invoices**.

---

## If a subscription expires unpaid

If an invoice is not paid by the due date:

1. Status may change to **Overdue** or **Suspended**.
2. **Domains**  risk of losing the name after registry grace periods; renew urgently.
3. **Hosting / email**  website or mail may stop working until payment.
4. **Servers**  VPS, VDS, or dedicated instances may be powered off or removed after a grace period.

**What to do:**

1. Open **Billing → Invoices**.
2. Pay the latest unpaid renewal invoice.
3. Wait up to 30 minutes for services to restore.
4. If still offline, contact support with invoice and subscription IDs.

---

## Cancel or change a plan

Cancellation and upgrade rules depend on the product:

- Some plans can be changed from **Marketplace** or by ordering a new plan on [nisccloud.com](https://www.nisccloud.com).
- To cancel before renewal, contact Nisc Cloud support with your subscription name and reason  the dashboard may not show a self-service cancel button for every product.

Always download paid invoices for your records before making billing changes.
