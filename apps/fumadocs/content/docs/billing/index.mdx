Billing in the customer dashboard covers **money you owe or have paid** for Nisc Cloud services — domain renewals, hosting, email, servers, marketing tools, and one-time orders.

All billing menus are under the **Billing** section in the left sidebar at [npanel.nisccloud.com](https://npanel.nisccloud.com).

---

## Two main billing screens

| Menu | What it shows |
|------|----------------|
| **Invoices** | Individual bills — new orders, renewals, manual charges. Pay or download PDF here. |
| **Subscriptions** | Recurring services with a renewal cycle (monthly or yearly). See next due date and status. |

Most customers use **Invoices** when they receive a payment reminder, and **Subscriptions** to plan ahead for renewals.

---

## How billing connects to ordering

1. You order on [nisccloud.com](https://www.nisccloud.com) or in **Marketplace** inside the dashboard.
2. After checkout, an **invoice** is created — usually marked paid if you paid immediately.
3. Recurring products also create a **subscription** with a renewal date.
4. Before renewal, you may receive email reminders and see a new **unpaid invoice** in the dashboard.

See [Checkout and payment](/docs/billing/checkout) for paying during an order.

---

## Pay an unpaid invoice

1. Sign in to the dashboard.
2. Click **Billing → Invoices**.
3. Find an invoice with status **Unpaid** or **Due**.
4. Click the invoice row to open it.
5. Review line items (domain, hosting, etc.) and total amount.
6. Click **Pay**, **Pay now**, or **Complete payment**.
7. Choose **M-Pesa**, **card**, or another offered method.
8. For M-Pesa, approve the prompt on your phone.
9. When payment succeeds, status changes to **Paid** and services stay active.

---

## M-Pesa payments

Kenyan customers often pay with M-Pesa:

1. At checkout or on an invoice, select **M-Pesa**.
2. Enter the phone number registered with M-Pesa if asked.
3. Wait for the STK push on your phone.
4. Enter your M-Pesa PIN to approve.
5. Do not close the browser until you see **Payment successful**.

If the STK push does not arrive, check the phone number and Safaricom network, then try again or contact support.

## Company details on invoices

Invoice headers use billing details from your team settings:

1. Go to **Account Settings → Settings**.
2. Open the **Billing** tab.
3. Update name and address, then save.

See [Settings and team](/docs/npanel/settings).

## If payment fails or services suspend

1. Open **Billing → Invoices** and retry payment on the latest unpaid invoice.
2. Confirm your M-Pesa balance or card limit.
3. If invoices show paid but a site is still offline, wait 15–30 minutes for provisioning, then contact support with the invoice number.

Keeping billing email current under [My Profile](/docs/npanel/profile) helps you receive renewal reminders before services expire.
