---
title: Checkout and payment
description: How to pay when ordering on nisccloud.com or the dashboard Marketplace with M-Pesa or card.
---

Checkout is where you **pay for new orders**  before services appear in your dashboard. You can checkout from the public website or from **Marketplace** inside the dashboard.

---

## Checkout on nisccloud.com

1. Browse [www.nisccloud.com](https://www.nisccloud.com) and choose a product (hosting, domain, email, etc.).
2. Click **Order**, **Get started**, or **Add to cart**.
3. Configure options (plan tier, domain name, billing period).
4. Open the **cart** or continue to **Checkout**.
5. **Sign in** or **create an account** if you are not logged in.
6. Review order summary  products, prices, and term length.
7. Enter or confirm **billing details** (name, address, phone).
8. Choose **payment method** (M-Pesa or card when available).
9. Complete payment (see below).
10. You receive confirmation on screen and usually by email.
11. Sign in at [npanel.nisccloud.com](https://npanel.nisccloud.com) to manage new services.

---

## Checkout in Marketplace

1. Sign in to the dashboard.
2. Click **More Services → Marketplace**.
3. Browse categories and add products to cart.
4. Proceed to **Checkout** inside the dashboard.
5. Confirm billing details and payment method.
6. Pay — services attach to your **current organization** automatically.

See [Marketplace](/docs/marketplace).

---

## Pay with M-Pesa

1. At payment step, select **M-Pesa** or **Lipa na M-Pesa**.
2. Enter the Safaricom number that receives STK pushes (often your own).
3. Click **Pay** or **Submit**.
4. On your phone, approve the **STK push** and enter your M-Pesa PIN.
5. Wait for success message in the browser  do not refresh early.
6. If failed, check network and balance, then retry once.

Successful M-Pesa payments create a **paid invoice** under **Billing → Invoices**.

---

## Pay with card

1. Select **Card** or **Credit/Debit card**.
2. Enter card number, expiry, and CVV on the secure payment page.
3. Complete any 3-D Secure or OTP step from your bank.
4. Wait for **Payment successful**.

---

## After checkout

| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| 1 | Check email for order confirmation |
| 2 | Open dashboard → **Billing → Invoices** for receipt |
| 3 | Open **Domains**, **Hosting**, **Emails**, etc. to confirm provisioning |
| 4 | Complete setup (DNS, mailboxes, WordPress) using linked docs |

Provisioning may take minutes. DNS worldwide can take up to 24–48 hours.

---

## Pay later (invoice checkout)

Some orders may create an **unpaid invoice** first:

1. Finish checkout without immediate payment if that option exists.
2. Go to **Billing → Invoices** in the dashboard.
3. Open the invoice and pay before the due date.

See [Invoices](/docs/billing/invoices).
