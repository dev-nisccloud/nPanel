**WordPress** powers millions of business websites and blogs. Nisc Cloud offers **WordPress hosting** and one-click install on shared hosting — all manageable from **Hosting** and **WordPress** menus.

---

## Where to manage WordPress

| Menu | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| **Hosting → [site] → WordPress** | Install WordPress on a hosting package |
| **WordPress** (sidebar) | List and open WordPress sites on your account |

Sign in at [npanel.nisccloud.com](https://npanel.nisccloud.com) to access both.

---

## Typical workflow

1. Order [WordPress hosting](/docs/hosting/wordpress-hosting) or shared hosting with WordPress support.
2. [Install WordPress](/docs/wordpress/install) on your domain.
3. Log in to `/wp-admin` and build your site.
4. [Manage](/docs/wordpress/manage) updates, plugins, and themes.
5. Set up [Backups](/docs/wordpress/backups) regularly.
6. [Migrate](/docs/wordpress/migrate) existing sites from another host if needed.

---

## Connect domain and SSL

1. Point domain via [Nameservers](/docs/domains/nameservers) or [DNS](/docs/domains/dns).
2. Enable [SSL](/docs/ssl/enable) for HTTPS.
3. In WordPress **Settings → General**, use `https://` URLs.
