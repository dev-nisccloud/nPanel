title: VDS hosting

A **VDS (Virtual Dedicated Server)** offers **dedicated resources** on enterprise hardware — more consistent performance than standard VPS when you need guaranteed CPU, RAM, and I/O for busy applications.

---

## Order a VDS

1. Visit [nisccloud.com](https://www.nisccloud.com) → **VDS** under hosting or cloud servers.
2. Compare plans by CPU, RAM, storage type (NVMe/SSD), and bandwidth.
3. Select operating system template.
4. Complete checkout.

Available from **Marketplace** after login as well.

---

## Open your VDS

1. Sign in at [npanel.nisccloud.com](https://npanel.nisccloud.com).
2. Click **Cloud Servers → VDS**.
3. Click your instance.

Dashboard shows IP address, power state, credentials, and management actions similar to VPS — start, stop, reboot, and console access where supported.

---

## Connect and configure

Use **SSH** like a VPS:

```bash
ssh root@YOUR_VDS_IP
```

1. Secure the server (updates, firewall, non-root user).
2. Deploy your application stack or control panel.
3. Point domain **A records** to the VDS IP in **Domains → DNS**.
4. Install SSL — see [Install SSL](/docs/ssl/install).

---

## When to choose VDS over VPS

Choose **VDS** when:

- Traffic spikes cause noisy-neighbor issues on VPS
- You need predictable performance for databases or ecommerce
- Compliance or SLA requires dedicated resource allocation

Choose **VPS** when cost matters more and workloads are lighter.

Choose **Dedicated** when you need an entire physical server.

---

## Billing

Renewals appear under **Billing → Subscriptions** and **Invoices**. Keep payment current — unpaid VDS may be suspended and data removed after policy grace period.
