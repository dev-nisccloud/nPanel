**Hosting** is where your website files, databases, and applications live on the internet. Nisc Cloud offers several hosting types for different needs  from simple websites to full servers you control yourself.

All hosting you own appears under **Cloud Services** in the customer dashboard.

---

## Hosting types

| Type | Best for | Where in dashboard |
|------|----------|-------------------|
| **Shared hosting** | Small business sites, brochures, blogs | **Hosting** |
| **WordPress hosting** | WordPress sites with optimized stack | **Hosting** and **WordPress** menus |
| **VPS** | Developers, custom apps, root access | **Cloud Servers → VPS** |
| **VDS** | Stronger isolation, heavier workloads | **Cloud Servers → VDS** |
| **Dedicated** | Maximum power, compliance, large traffic | **Cloud Servers → Dedicated** |

Order any plan on [nisccloud.com](https://www.nisccloud.com) or **Marketplace**, then manage it in the dashboard.

---

## Open shared or WordPress hosting

1. Sign in at [npanel.nisccloud.com](https://npanel.nisccloud.com).
2. Click **Hosting** in the sidebar.
3. Click your hosting package or site name in the list.
4. Use tabs: **Overview**, **DNS**, **Mail**, **File Manager**, **Subdomains**, **Databases**, **WordPress**, **Backup**.

Full tab guide: [Manage hosting](/docs/hosting/manage-hosting).

---

## Open cloud servers (VPS, VDS, dedicated)

1. Click **Cloud Servers** in the sidebar.
2. Click **VPS**, **VDS**, or **Dedicated**.
3. Click a server in the list to view IP address, status, credentials, and controls.

---

## Choose the right plan

**Shared hosting**  lowest cost, easiest setup, good for one or few websites without server admin skills.

**WordPress hosting**  shared environment tuned for WordPress (updates, staging, one-click install).

**VPS**  your own virtual machine; you install software and manage security; scalable CPU/RAM.

**VDS**  dedicated resources on virtual hardware; middle ground between VPS and bare metal.

**Dedicated**  physical server for your exclusive use; highest performance and control.

## Connect a domain to hosting

1. Register or transfer domain under **Domains**.
2. In hosting **Overview**, copy **nameservers** or **IP address**.
3. On the domain, set [Nameservers](/docs/domains/nameservers) or [DNS A record](/docs/domains/dns).
4. Enable [SSL](/docs/ssl) for HTTPS.

---

## Billing

Hosting renews on a schedule shown under **Billing → Subscriptions**. Pay renewal **Invoices** before due date to avoid suspension.
