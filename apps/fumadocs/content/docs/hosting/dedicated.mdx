Dedicated servers provide exclusive hardware for workloads that need consistent performance or specialized configs.

## When to choose dedicated

- High CPU / RAM / disk requirements
- Compliance or isolation needs
- Large databases or media platforms

## Onboarding

After order confirmation, you receive access details (IP, credentials or IPMI info as applicable). Use nPanel for billing, tickets, and reboots where available.

Contact support for custom networking, RAID, or OS reinstalls.
