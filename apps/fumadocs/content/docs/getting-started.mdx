This guide explains how to go from browsing [nisccloud.com](https://www.nisccloud.com) to managing your services inside the customer dashboard.

## What you need before you start

- A valid email address you can access (used for login and invoices)
- A phone number (helpful for account recovery and M-Pesa payments in Kenya)
- For domain registration: accurate contact details for WHOIS (the public registry record for your domain)

You do **not** need technical experience. The dashboard is built so you can register a domain, buy hosting, and set up email by following the menus.

---

## Step 1: Browse services on the website

1. Open [www.nisccloud.com](https://www.nisccloud.com).
2. Use the top navigation to explore what you need:
   - **Hosting** — shared, WordPress, VPS, VDS, dedicated servers
   - **Domains** — search and register, or transfer an existing domain
   - **Email** — business email on your domain
   - **Digital marketing** — SEO, ads, email marketing, and more
3. When you find a plan or product, click **Get started**, **Order**, or **Add to cart** (the exact button depends on the page).

You can order multiple items in one checkout (for example, a domain plus shared hosting plus email).

---

## Step 2: Create an account or sign in at checkout

When you checkout, you will be asked to sign in or create an account:

1. If you **already have an account**, enter your email and password and continue.
2. If you are **new**, follow the sign-up form:
   - Enter your name and email
   - Choose a strong password
   - Complete any verification steps (such as confirming your email)

After payment succeeds, Nisc Cloud provisions your services. This can take a few minutes for hosting and email, and up to 24–48 hours for some domain or DNS changes worldwide.

---

## Step 3: Open your customer dashboard

Your dashboard is where you manage everything after you buy:

1. Go to [https://npanel.nisccloud.com](https://npanel.nisccloud.com).
2. Sign in with the same email and password you used at checkout.
3. If you belong to more than one company or team, pick the correct one from the list after login.
4. You land on **Home** — the main dashboard screen.

The **left sidebar** is your map. It is grouped into sections:

| Sidebar section | What it is for |
|-----------------|----------------|
| **Cloud Services** | Domains, hosting, WordPress, email, cloud servers |
| **Digital Marketing** | Ads, email marketing, SEO, creatives |
| **Gen AI Tools** | Zeepo AI Agent |
| **Billing** | Invoices and subscriptions |
| **Account Settings** | Your profile and company settings |
| **More Services** | Marketplace |

See [Navigation in the dashboard](/docs/npanel/navigation) for a full menu-by-menu walkthrough.

---

## Step 4: Confirm your order appeared

After your first purchase, check that services show up:

**Domains**

1. In the left sidebar, click **Domains**.
2. Click **My Domains**.
3. You should see your registered or transferred domain listed.

**Hosting**

1. Click **Hosting** in the sidebar.
2. You should see your hosting package or site listed. Click it to open **Overview**, **File Manager**, **DNS**, and other tabs.

**Email**

1. Click **Emails** in the sidebar.
2. Open your email plan to create mailboxes and view webmail settings.

**Invoices**

1. Click **Invoices** under **Billing**.
2. Open the latest invoice to confirm amount paid and renewal dates.

---

## Step 5: Complete first-time setup tasks

Depending on what you bought, do these next:

| You bought | Do this next |
|------------|--------------|
| Domain only | [Point DNS or nameservers](/docs/domains/dns) to your website or hosting |
| Domain + hosting | Open hosting **Overview** and note nameservers or use **DNS** tab to connect the domain |
| Business email | [Create mailboxes](/docs/email/setup) and [set up your phone or Outlook](/docs/email/clients) |
| WordPress hosting | [Install or open WordPress](/docs/wordpress/install) from the hosting or WordPress menu |
| VPS / VDS / dedicated | Open **Cloud Servers** in the sidebar and follow the server overview for access details |

---

## Paying with M-Pesa or card

Kenyan customers can often pay with **M-Pesa** during checkout. Card payments may also be available depending on the product.

1. At checkout on nisccloud.com, choose your payment method.
2. For M-Pesa, approve the STK push on your phone when prompted.
3. After payment, return to the dashboard and check **Billing → Invoices** for a paid receipt.

Future renewals may appear under **Billing → Subscriptions**. See [Billing overview](/docs/billing) for paying invoices and managing renewals.
