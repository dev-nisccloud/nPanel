Get up and running on Nisc Cloud in a few steps.

## 1. Create an account

1. Go to [nisccloud.com](https://nisccloud.com) and sign up.
2. Verify your email address.
3. Sign in to [nPanel](https://npanel.nisccloud.com).

## 2. Choose a service

From nPanel you can order:

| Service | Typical use |
|---------|-------------|
| WordPress Hosting | Blogs, business sites, WooCommerce |
| Shared / Website Hosting | General websites and apps |
| Domains | Registration, transfer, DNS |
| VPS / VDS | Custom stacks and higher control |
| Business Email | Professional `@yourdomain` mail |
| SSL | HTTPS for your sites |

## 3. Complete checkout

Follow the order flow, choose a plan and billing cycle, then complete payment. Your service appears in nPanel once provisioning finishes.
