---
title: Transfer a domain
description: Move an existing domain to Nisc Cloud.
---

## Before you start

- Unlock the domain at the current registrar
- Disable WHOIS privacy temporarily (if required)
- Obtain the **EPP / auth code**
- Confirm the domain is older than 60 days (ICANN rule for many TLDs)

## Steps

1. In nPanel, go to **Domains** → **Transfer**.
2. Enter the domain and auth code.
3. Complete checkout.
4. Approve the transfer email if your current registrar sends one.

Transfers typically take 5-7 days for gTLDs. You can track status in nPanel.
