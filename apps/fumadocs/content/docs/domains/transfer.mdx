---
title: Transfer a domain
description: Move a domain you already own from another registrar into your Nisc Cloud account.
---

A **domain transfer** moves management of a name you **already own** from another registrar (GoDaddy, Namecheap, etc.) to Nisc Cloud. After transfer, you renew and manage DNS through the customer dashboard.

---

## Before you start

Gather these from your **current registrar**:

1. **Domain unlocked**  turn off registrar lock / transfer lock in their control panel.
2. **Authorization code (EPP code)**  request it from the old registrar (sometimes emailed).
3. **Valid admin email**  the registry sends approval emails to the domain contact.
4. **Domain older than 60 days**  ICANN rules block transfer of newly registered domains in most cases.
5. **No recent transfer**  if you transferred in the last 60 days, wait before transferring again.

Keep the domain **active** at the old registrar until the transfer completes  do not let it expire mid-transfer.

---

## Start transfer from the dashboard

1. Sign in at [npanel.nisccloud.com](https://npanel.nisccloud.com).
2. Click **Domains** in the sidebar.
3. Click **Transfer**.
4. Enter the full domain name (e.g. `example.co.ke`).
5. Enter the **authorization (EPP) code** from your old registrar.
6. Add to cart and proceed to checkout.
7. Pay the transfer fee (often includes one year extension).
8. Confirm contact email is correct for approval messages.

---

## Start transfer from nisccloud.com

1. Visit the **Domain transfer** page on [nisccloud.com](https://www.nisccloud.com).
2. Enter domain name and auth code in the transfer tool.
3. Sign in and complete checkout.
4. Track progress in **Domains → My Domains** after login.

---

## Approve the transfer

1. Watch the **admin email** on the domainNisc Cloud's WHOIS contact.
2. Open emails from the registry or old registrar and **approve** the transfer if asked.
3. Some TLDs complete automatically after 5–7 days if not rejected.
4. In the dashboard, open **My Domains**  status may show **Pending transfer** until finished.

Transfer usually completes in **5–7 days** for gTLDs like `.com`; some ccTLDs may differ.

---

## After transfer completes

1. Open **Domains → My Domains** and click the domain.
2. Verify **Overview** shows Nisc Cloud as registrar and new expiry date.
3. Set **Nameservers** or **DNS** to point to your hosting:
   - Nisc Cloud hosting  use nameservers shown in hosting **Overview**
   - External hosting  update A/CNAME records in **DNS** tab
4. Re-enable **privacy / protect brand** if you use those features.

Your website and email should keep working if DNS/nameservers stayed the same during transfer. If you changed nameservers at the old registrar right before transfer, update them again at Nisc Cloud.

---

## Common problems

| Problem | What to do |
|---------|------------|
| Invalid auth code | Regenerate EPP code at old registrar; codes expire |
| Domain locked | Disable transfer lock at old registrar |
| Transfer rejected | Contact old registrar; ensure email approval was clicked |
| Email not received | Update WHOIS email at old registrar and retry |
