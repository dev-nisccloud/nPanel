---
title: Nameservers
description: Point your domain to Nisc Cloud nameservers or custom nameservers from another DNS provider.
---

**Nameservers** decide which companyNisc Cloud's DNS system controls your domain. When you use Nisc Cloud nameservers, you edit records in **Domains → My Domains → DNS** or in **Hosting → DNS**. When you use Cloudflare or another hostNisc Cloud's nameservers, you edit DNS at that provider instead.

---

## Open the nameservers settings

1. Sign in at [npanel.nisccloud.com](https://npanel.nisccloud.com).
2. Click **Domains → My Domains**.
3. Click your **domain name**.
4. Click the **Nameservers** tab.
5. You see current nameserver hostnames and options to change them.

---

## Use Nisc Cloud nameservers (recommended with Nisc Cloud hosting)

Best when your **website and email** are hosted with Nisc Cloud and you want one place to manage DNS.

1. Open your **Hosting** account → **Overview** tab.
2. Note the **nameservers** listed (often two or more hostnames like `ns1.example.nisccloud.com`).
3. Go to domain **Nameservers** tab.
4. Choose **Custom nameservers** or **Use default** if the dashboard fills Nisc Cloud NS automatically.
5. Enter each nameserver hostname exactly as shown in hosting Overview.
6. Save changes.
7. Wait **24–48 hours** for propagation (many updates work within a few hours).

After propagation, manage records on the domain **DNS** tab or hosting **DNS** tab.

---

## Use custom nameservers (external DNS)

Use when **Cloudflare**, **Google Domains DNS**, or your developer manages DNS elsewhere.

1. On **Nameservers** tab, select **Custom nameservers**.
2. Remove old Nisc Cloud nameserver entries if present.
3. Enter the nameserver hostnames from your external provider (usually 2–4 names).
4. Save.
5. At the **external provider**, recreate all A, MX, CNAME, and TXT records your site and email need.

Your domain **DNS tab in Nisc Cloud may no longer apply** until you switch nameservers back.

---

## Switch from external back to Nisc Cloud

1. Copy or export records from the external DNS provider first.
2. Set Nisc Cloud nameservers on the **Nameservers** tab.
3. After propagation, re-add records on **DNS** tab in the dashboard.
4. Test website and email.

---

## Check if nameserver change worked

1. Use an online DNS lookup tool (search “DNS propagation check”) and query **NS** records for your domain.
2. Compare results to the nameservers you set.
3. If old nameservers still appear everywhere after 48 hours, confirm you saved correctly and contact support.
