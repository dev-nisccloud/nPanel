When you click a domain under **Domains → My Domains**, you open the **domain portfolio** — a detail page with tabs for everything about that one name.

---

## Open a domain

1. Sign in at [npanel.nisccloud.com](https://npanel.nisccloud.com).
2. Click **Domains** in the sidebar.
3. Click **My Domains**.
4. Find your domain in the list (use search if many domains).
5. Click the **domain name** (e.g. `yourcompany.co.ke`).
6. The portfolio page opens with tabs across the top.

---

## Overview tab

The **Overview** tab is the summary screen.

You typically see:

- **Domain status** — Active, expired, pending transfer, etc.
- **Registration date** and **expiry / renewal date**
- **Registrar** information
- **Auto-renewal** or renewal reminders (if shown)
- Shortcuts to renew or open related hosting

**What to do here**

- Check expiry date and pay renewal invoices under **Billing → Invoices** before the date passes.
- Note whether the domain uses Nisc Cloud nameservers or external DNS (often shown on Overview or Nameservers tab).

---

## DNS tab

The **DNS** tab is where you add and edit **DNS records** — the instructions that send visitors to your website and mail to your inbox.

Common record types:

| Type | Used for |
|------|----------|
| **A** | Point domain to a server IP address |
| **CNAME** | Alias one name to another (e.g. `www` to main site) |
| **MX** | Mail servers for email |
| **TXT** | Verification, SPF, DKIM for email and SSL |

Step-by-step editing is in [DNS records](/docs/domains/dns).

---

## Nameservers tab

The **Nameservers** tab controls **which companyNisc Cloud's DNS system** answers lookups for your domain.

**Default Nisc Cloud nameservers** — use when hosting and DNS are with Nisc Cloud. Hosting **Overview** may list the exact hostnames to use.

**Custom nameservers** — use when another company (Cloudflare, external host) manages DNS. Enter their nameserver hostnames here.

Full steps: [Nameservers](/docs/domains/nameservers).

---

## Protect Brand tab

The **Protect Brand** tab includes options to help protect your domain and brand online — for example monitoring or services related to trademark and domain security depending on your plan.

1. Open the **Protect Brand** tab.
2. Read each offered option on screen.
3. Enable or order features as needed.
4. Save changes.

Availability varies by TLD and product. If the tab is empty, the feature may not apply to that extension.

---

## Typical workflows

**Connect domain to Nisc Cloud hosting**

1. Open domain → **Nameservers** → set Nisc Cloud nameservers from hosting **Overview**.
2. Or keep nameservers and use **DNS** → add A record to hosting IP.

**Connect domain to business email**

1. Open domain → **DNS**.
2. Add **MX** records shown in **Emails** plan overview.
3. Add **TXT** for SPF/DKIM if provided.

**Point domain to external website builder**

1. Open **DNS**.
2. Add A or CNAME records from your builderNisc Cloud's instructions (Shopify, Wix, etc.).
