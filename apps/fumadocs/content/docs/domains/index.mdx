A **domain name** is your address on the internet — for example `yourbusiness.co.ke` or `yourbrand.com`. Nisc Cloud lets you **register** new domains, **transfer** domains from other companies, and **manage** DNS and nameservers from the customer dashboard.

---

## Where domains live in the dashboard

1. Sign in at [npanel.nisccloud.com](https://npanel.nisccloud.com).
2. In the left sidebar under **Cloud Services**, click **Domains**.
3. You see three sub-menus:

| Sub-menu | Purpose |
|----------|---------|
| **Get a Domain** | Search and buy a new domain |
| **Transfer** | Bring a domain you own elsewhere to Nisc Cloud |
| **My Domains** | List and manage domains already in your account |

---

## Register vs transfer

**Register** when the name is available and nobody owns it yet (or you want a new TLD alongside an existing site).

**Transfer** when you already own the domain at another registrar and want Nisc Cloud to be the registrar going forward. You need the domain unlocked and an authorization (EPP) code from the old provider.

---

## After you own a domain

Click **My Domains**, then click a domain name to open its detail page with tabs:

| Tab | Purpose |
|-----|---------|
| **Overview** | Registration dates, status, and summary |
| **DNS** | A, CNAME, MX, TXT records pointing the domain to website and email |
| **Nameservers** | Which DNS servers control the domain (Nisc Cloud or external) |
| **Protect Brand** | Brand protection options where available |

See [Domain portfolio](/docs/domains/portfolio) for a full walkthrough of each tab.

---

## Order domains on the website

You can also start from [nisccloud.com](https://www.nisccloud.com):

1. Open **Domains** in the top menu or visit the domain search page.
2. Type the name you want and search.
3. Add available extensions (`.co.ke`, `.com`, etc.) to cart.
4. Checkout and pay.
5. The domain appears under **Domains → My Domains** in the dashboard.

## Renewal and billing

Domains renew yearly (or on the term you purchased). Renewal invoices appear under **Billing → Invoices**. Active domain subscriptions show under **Billing → Subscriptions**. Pay before expiry to avoid losing the name.

See [Billing](/docs/billing).
