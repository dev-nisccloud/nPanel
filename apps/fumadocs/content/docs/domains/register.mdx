---
title: Register a domain
description: Search for and register a new domain name.
---

## Steps

1. Open [nPanel](https://npanel.nisccloud.com) → **Domains** → **Register**.
2. Search for your desired name (e.g. `example.com`).
3. Choose an available TLD and registration term.
4. Complete WHOIS / contact details accurately.
5. Checkout and confirm payment.

Your domain appears under **Domains** after registration completes. Propagation of nameservers can take up to 24-48 hours.

## After registration

- Point the domain to hosting via [DNS](/docs/domains/dns)
- Enable privacy protection if offered for your TLD
- Set a calendar reminder for renewal
