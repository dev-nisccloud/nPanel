This guide explains how to **register a new domain name** — when the name is available for purchase — using the customer dashboard or the Nisc Cloud website.

---

## Before you register

- Have accurate **contact details** ready (registries require real WHOIS information).
- Decide which **extension** you want (`.co.ke` for Kenya, `.com` for global, etc.).
- If you also need a website, consider ordering **hosting** in the same checkout so setup is easier later.

---

## Register from the dashboard

1. Sign in at [npanel.nisccloud.com](https://npanel.nisccloud.com).
2. In the left sidebar, click **Domains**.
3. Click **Get a Domain**.
4. In the search box, type the name **without** `www` — for example `mybusiness` or `mybusiness.co.ke`.
5. Click **Search** or press Enter.
6. Review results:
   - **Available** names can be added to cart.
   - **Taken** names cannot be registered unless the owner sells them.
7. Select the extensions you want (checkbox or **Add** button).
8. Choose registration **period** (often 1–5 years).
9. Proceed to **Checkout** or **Cart**.
10. Confirm billing details and pay with M-Pesa or card.
11. After payment succeeds, open **Domains → My Domains** — your domain should appear within a few minutes.

---

## Register from nisccloud.com

1. Go to [www.nisccloud.com](https://www.nisccloud.com) and open the **Domains** section or domain search on the homepage.
2. Enter your desired name and search.
3. Add available domains to cart.
4. Sign in or create an account at checkout.
5. Pay and wait for confirmation email.
6. Manage the domain in the dashboard under **My Domains**.

---

## Complete WHOIS / contact information

Registries require owner contact data on file:

1. During checkout, fill in registrant name, organization, email, phone, and address.
2. Use an email you monitor — renewal and transfer notices go there.
3. For `.co.ke` and other country domains, local presence rules may apply; follow prompts on screen.

Inaccurate data can block registration or cause compliance issues later.

---

## After registration

**Find your domain**

1. Click **Domains → My Domains**.
2. Click the new domain name.

**Point it to a website**

- If you bought Nisc Cloud hosting, use [Nameservers](/docs/domains/nameservers) or [DNS](/docs/domains/dns) as instructed in hosting **Overview**.
- If hosting is elsewhere, set nameservers or DNS records at the other hostNisc Cloud's instructions.

**Set up email**

- If you bought business email, create mailboxes under **Emails** and add MX records in domain **DNS**. See [Email DNS records](/docs/email/dns-records).

**Timeline**

- The domain is yours in the dashboard immediately after registration completes.
- DNS changes worldwide can take **24–48 hours** to fully propagate.

---

## Tips

- Register common misspellings or `.co.ke` + `.com` together if you protect a brand.
- Enable renewal reminders by keeping billing email current in [My Profile](/docs/npanel/profile).
- Open **Protect Brand** on the domain if offered for your TLD.
