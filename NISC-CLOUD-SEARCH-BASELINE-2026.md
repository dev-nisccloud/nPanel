## Manual public-search checks

On 2026-11-03, a live Chrome Google search for **business email hosting kenya** showed an AI Overview and a first results page. Google marked the results personalised and located the session in Kenya. Nisc Cloud was not observed in that first-page result set or in the expanded AI Overview. The overview included providers such as Hostraha, Truehost and HostAfrica. This is one dated, personalised sample; it does not prove absence in all locations, sessions, prompts or engines.

Observed competitor destinations:
- https://www.hostafrica.ke/email-hosting/
- https://truehost.co.ke/hosting/email/
- https://hostraha.co.ke/business-email

The result snippets and answer emphasize custom-domain addresses, mailbox/storage limits, pricing/billing terms, security, support and M-Pesa. This suggests useful information to cover accurately on Nisc Cloud's email service page; it is not proof of ranking causation.

Additional public search queries checked through the search tool: `"business email hosting kenya" "nisc"`, `"Nisc Cloud" SEO marketing hosting`, `"Nisc Cloud" "email hosting"`, and `best business email hosting Kenya`. Returned evidence included Nisc Cloud's own About page with service descriptions and third-party/provider comparison pages. This pass did not establish an independent recommendation of Nisc Cloud for business email. Branded retrieval of one's own page is not equivalent to an unprompted AI recommendation.

## Priorities based on evidence

1. Preserve VPS and other valuable .php routes through explicit intent-matched redirects and strong equivalent content.
2. Build email-hosting content around real packages, registration/renewal cost, limits, migration, deliverability setup, security and support. Target the observed Kenya/East Africa variants naturally; avoid stuffing or unsupported performance claims.
3. Strengthen dedicated hosting and cloud-provider content with verified technical and operational evidence.
4. Develop service-specific SEO/PPC content and case studies; current search exposure is modest and needs proof as well as copy.
5. Correct company identity and audit login/indexation noise; protect staging from competing with the public website.
6. Complete page exports, country/device segmentation and recent-period comparisons. Investigate the January/early-February reporting gap. Run a repeatable multi-engine prompt sample before claiming broad GEO coverage.
